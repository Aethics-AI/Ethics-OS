# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this community open, welcoming, diverse and
healthy.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people;
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community, not just for us as individuals.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## A note specific to this project

This project measures bias and fairness in language models. Discussions here
will touch on race, gender, disability, religion and sexuality as *subjects of
measurement* — that is the work, and technical disagreement about how to measure
them is welcome and necessary.

Using that subject matter as cover for disparaging people is not the same thing,
and will be treated as harassment. The distinction is usually obvious in
practice: a critique of a benchmark's construct validity is engineering, and a
generalisation about a group of people is not.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and also when someone is representing the
project in public — using an official email address, posting from an official
account, or acting as an appointed representative.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to
**conduct@aethics.com**.

This address is separate from `security@aethics.com` on purpose: the two need
different readers, and a conduct report may concern a maintainer. If your report
concerns whoever monitors this address, or you would rather not use it, contact
any maintainer directly and ask them to route it — you are not obliged to send a
report through the person it is about.

**Expect an acknowledgement within 3 working days.** Reports are handled
confidentially; the reporter's identity is not shared with the person reported
without the reporter's consent.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
are obliged to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

### Consequences

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in deciding a response:

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining why the behaviour was
   inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with those
   involved for a specified period, including in project spaces and externally.
3. **Temporary ban** — no public or private interaction with the community for a
   specified period.
4. **Permanent ban** — a pattern of violation, harassment of an individual, or
   aggression toward classes of individuals.
